Botanical Beach + Spinnakers + Beacon Hill
⏰ Start: 8:00 AM
Tidal Pools
Botanical Beach Provincial Park — world-class tidal pools, Port Renfrew, BC
Morning
☕
8:00 AM — Victoria Morning Coffee
TBD
Find a cozy Victoria coffee shop to kick off the day.
🚘
~8:30 AM — Drive to Port Renfrew
Approximately 1.5 hours from Victoria along the scenic West Coast Road. Aim to arrive around 10:00 AM.
Midday — ~10:00 AM to ~1:00 PM
🌊
Botanical Beach Provincial Park
Highlight
One of the most spectacular tidal pool ecosystems on the Pacific Coast. Explore sea stars, anemones, urchins, and incredible marine life in natural rock pools carved by the Pacific Ocean. Plan on about 3 hours at the tide pools — enough time to explore, but leaves room for a relaxed afternoon back in Victoria.
📌 Check tide tables before going — low tide is essential! Wear sturdy, closed-toe shoes with good grip.
🔗 Visitor Guide
Afternoon — Return to Victoria (~1:30–2:00 PM)
🚘
~1:00 PM — Return to Victoria
Drive back to Victoria (~1.5 hrs). Arrive early-to-mid afternoon, ready for lunch or an early dinner.
🍺
🍺 Spinnakers Gastro Brewpub & Guesthouses
🍺 Kirk’s / Dad’s Pick
Canada’s oldest brewpub (est. 1984) — right on Victoria’s waterfront. Brewed-on-site beers, locally-sourced food, and an oceanfront setting that’s hard to beat. Perfect for a late lunch or early dinner after returning from Port Renfrew around 1:30–2:00 PM.
📌 Reservation recommended for lunch/dinner.
🔗 spinnakers.com
Late Afternoon
🌿
🌿 Beacon Hill Park — Victoria
Victoria’s signature 200+ acre waterfront park — gardens, ponds, peacocks roaming freely, and sweeping ocean views from Dallas Road. A gentle, beautiful way to wind down the afternoon after a big day at the coast.
🔗 Beacon Hill Park Info
🏠
Evening — Back to VRBO
Relax at the VRBO after a full day. Spinnakers covers dinner — no need to go back out.

Gardens Day + Fairmont Empress
⏰ Start: 8:00 AM
Butterflies & Blooms
The Butchart Gardens — the legendary Sunken Garden, Brentwood Bay, BC
Morning
☕
8:00 AM — Coffee / Breakfast
TBD
A relaxed Victoria morning before the drive to the peninsula.
🚘
~9:00 AM — Drive to Brentwood Bay
About a 30-minute drive north from Victoria to the Saanich Peninsula gardens.
🦋
Victoria Butterfly Gardens
🦋 Megan’s Pick
A tropical indoor conservatory in Brentwood Bay filled with free-flying exotic butterflies, lush hanging flowers, and vibrant tropical plants. A magical, immersive experience for all ages.
🔗 butterflygardens.com
Afternoon
🌻
The Butchart Gardens
Must-See
One of the world’s most celebrated gardens — the legendary Sunken Garden, Rose Garden, Japanese Garden, and Italian Garden. Just 2 km from Butterfly Gardens. Spectacular in September with late-season blooms.
🔗 butchartgardens.com
☕
Afternoon Tea — The Dining Room Restaurant
Book Now! 🦋 Megan’s Highlight
Megan’s highlight — the legendary Butchart Gardens afternoon tea experience. Elegant, historic, and utterly delightful. This fills up months in advance.
⚠️ BOOK IN ADVANCE — this is a must-reserve experience!
🔗 Reserve at butchartgardens.com
⏱️
Pacing Note
Budget 2-3 hours total between the two gardens. Enjoy it, but don’t try to see every corner — this is meant to be a lovely, relaxed day.
Evening — Flexible Dinner
🏛
🏛 Fairmont Empress — Dinner Option
Preferred Dinner
Victoria’s most iconic landmark — the Fairmont Empress on the Inner Harbour. The Q Restaurant & Bar or Q Bar is a landmark Victoria experience if the group has energy left after the gardens. Alternatively, the Empress is world-famous for its afternoon tea — if you’d prefer the classic Empress tea experience instead of Butchart’s tea, swap the afternoon plan.
📌 Reservations recommended — tea especially fills up. The Empress tea is a world-famous experience in its own right.
🔗 Empress Dining
🏠
Fallback — Cook-In at the VRBO
Alternative
After a full gardens day, a relaxed cook-in night at the VRBO is a perfectly lovely option too. Open some wine, put your feet up.

Travel Day to North Vancouver + Capilano + Ramen

Ferry + Capilano
Capilano Suspension Bridge — 137 metres above the Capilano River, North Vancouver
🍟 Poutine Watch — Keep an eye out for poutine spots for Megan throughout Vancouver!
Morning
📦
8:00 AM — Check Out of Victoria VRBO
Pack up, grab coffee [TBD], and say goodbye to our charming Victoria home base. Drive to Swartz Bay Ferry Terminal (~30 min north of Victoria).

Late Morning — BC Ferries: Swartz Bay to Tsawwassen
Time TBD
The scenic BC Ferries crossing through the Gulf Islands — one of the most beautiful ferry routes in the world. Approximately 1.5 hours. Walk the decks and enjoy the views.
🔗 bcferries.com
Early Afternoon
🚗
Drive Tsawwassen → North Vancouver
About 45 minutes via Highway 99 and the Lions Gate Bridge.
Afternoon
🌳
Capilano Suspension Bridge Park
Book Now!
Walk the iconic 137-metre suspension bridge swaying above the Capilano River gorge. Then explore the Treetops Adventure (suspension bridges through old-growth forest canopy) and the thrilling Cliffwalk along granite canyon walls. Budget 1-2 hours here — focused on the main bridge and a quick treetop walk after a long travel day.
⚠️ BUY TICKETS IN ADVANCE online — timed entry fills up fast in September!
🔗 capbridge.com
⛰️
⛰️ Grouse Mountain — Optional Hike
Optional — If Time Allows
Located just ~5 minutes from Capilano Bridge — they’re practically neighbors. The famous Grouse Grind is a 2.9 km, 2–3 hour vertical hike nicknamed “Mother Nature’s Stairmaster.” If the group has energy and time after the ferry and Capilano visit, this is a spectacular add-on. The Skyride gondola is a less-strenuous alternative with equally great summit views.
Skip this if the group is tired from the travel day — it’ll still be a great day without it.
🔗 grousemountain.com
Late Afternoon / Evening
🏠
Check In — North Vancouver VRBO
VRBO TBD
Drop bags, freshen up, and gear up for a well-earned dinner.
🍜
Dinner — Ramen Danbo
Travel Day Treat 🍜 Nancy’s / Mom’s Pick
Authentic Fukuoka-style tonkotsu ramen. Rich, deeply flavored pork bone broth, fully customizable. A satisfying and deeply restorative end to a long travel day.
🔗 ramendanbo.com

Stanley Park E-bikes + Teahouse + Granville Island
⏰ Start: 8:00 AM
Vancouver Icons
🍟 Poutine Watch — Keep an eye out for poutine spots for Megan throughout Vancouver!
Morning
☕
8:00 AM — Coffee
TBD
Fuel up for a full Vancouver day.
🚗
~9:00 AM — Drive Across Lions Gate Bridge
~20 minutes from North Vancouver into Stanley Park.
🌳
Stanley Park — Vancouver
Highlight
One of North America’s greatest urban parks. The 9 km seawall is ideal for e-bikes — a relaxed, scenic lap with stunning views of English Bay and the North Shore mountains. Visit the totem poles, explore old-growth forest, and soak in the autumn foliage.
🔗 Stanley Park Info
🚲
🚲 E-bike Rental — Stanley Park Seawall
The 9 km seawall loop is perfect on e-bikes — much less tiring than walking the whole circuit, and you can stop for photos anywhere. Rental shops near the park entrance (e.g., Spokes Bicycle Rentals, Cycle City Rentals). Budget 2–3 hours for a relaxed lap with stops.
📌 Consider reserving e-bikes in advance for the morning slot — popular in September.
Lunch / Wine Hour
🍷
The Teahouse Restaurant — Stanley Park
Reserve!
A historic English Bay landmark inside Stanley Park. Stunning ocean and mountain views. Recommended for brunch or the wine hour — dinner is pricey but the setting is unmatched.
📌 RESERVATIONS RECOMMENDED.
🔗 Reserve at vancouverdine.com
Afternoon — Granville Island
⛵️
E-bike or Sea-Taxi to Granville Island
Two fun options from Stanley Park to Granville Island: (1) Continue on e-bikes along the Seaside Greenway (~30 min scenic ride across False Creek), or (2) Aquabus / False Creek Ferries — a charming 5-minute harbour ride. Both are scenic — group’s choice based on energy.
🏪
Granville Island Public Market
One of North America’s great public markets — fresh produce, local food artisans, crafts, and grab-and-go stalls with waterfront seating. Great for a browse and a casual lunch bite before or after the brewery.
🔗 granvilleisland.com/public-market
🍺
Granville Island Brewing
Flexible Meal 🍺 Kirk’s / Dad’s Pick
Canada’s original craft brewery (est. 1984). Lunch OR dinner at the taproom — use flexibly depending on how the Teahouse pacing goes. Right next to the Public Market — pair them naturally.
🔗 granvilleislandbrewing.ca
🏠
Return to North Vancouver VRBO
Wind down after a big Vancouver day.

Wednesday, September 17, 2026
Kitsilano Beach + The Naam + Night Market
⏰ Start: 8:00 AM
City Day
Richmond Night Market — one of North America’s largest night markets, Richmond, BC
🍟 Poutine Watch — Keep an eye out for poutine spots for Megan throughout Vancouver! The Night Market almost certainly has poutine vendors.
Morning
☕
8:00 AM — Morning Coffee
TBD
A deliberately slow morning — busy evening ahead with the Night Market, so lean into the calm.
🏖
🏖 Kitsilano Beach — Morning Walk
Famous Vancouver beach with the iconic Kits Pool (outdoor saltwater pool), beach volleyball courts, and sweeping views of downtown Vancouver and the North Shore mountains. A perfect West Coast morning — coffee walk along the beach, soak up the energy.
Brunch / Lunch
🌿
The Naam — Kitsilano
Vancouver Institution
Vancouver institution since 1968 — vegetarian with Thai influence, open 24 hours. Right in the Kitsilano neighborhood, just steps from the beach. A casual, leisurely brunch or lunch to contrast with the Night Market’s food-stall chaos later in the evening.
🔗 thenaam.com
Afternoon — Vancouver Wander
🏗️
Vancouver at Leisure
Suggestions: Gastown (historic cobblestone streets, the steam clock, indie shops), Downtown / Waterfront (Canada Place, the harbour, views of the mountains). Just wander and enjoy — Poutine Watch active for any afternoon stops.
Evening — 3 to 4 Hours
🌅
Richmond Night Market
Highlight
One of North America’s largest and most vibrant night markets. Hundreds of food stalls, games, live entertainment, and dazzling lights. An unforgettable sensory experience — Asian street food, international bites, and festive atmosphere. Budget 3-4 hours to really enjoy it.
⚠️ NOTE: Confirm Wednesday availability — the market typically runs Friday–Sunday. If Wed is not available, shift to an available Fri/Sat.
🔗 richmondnightmarket.com

Tim Hortons + Chinatown BBQ + I-5 Home

Homeward Bound

☕ Tim Hortons — Classic Canadian Send-Off
Quintessential Canadian morning stop on the way out. Timbits (the famous donut holes) + coffee — the perfect farewell to Canada. Tim Hortons locations are all along the route south, so it’s easy to hit one before the border crossing.

🍜 Chinatown BBQ — Family-Style Send-Off
Farewell Lunch
Family-style Cantonese roasted meats — duck, pork belly, char siu. A communal, delicious send-off in Vancouver’s historic Chinatown before leaving the city entirely. Perfect for a party of four.
