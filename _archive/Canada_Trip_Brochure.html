TBD

~8:30 AM — Drive to Port Renfrew
Approximately 1.5 hours from Victoria along the scenic West Coast Road. Aim to arrive around 10:00 AM.
Midday — ~10:00 AM to ~1:00 PM
🌊
Botanical Beach Provincial Park
Highlight
One of the most spectacular tidal pool ecosystems on the Pacific Coast. Explore sea stars, anemones, urchins, and incredible marine life in natural rock pools carved by the Pacific Ocean. Plan on about 3 hours at the tide pools — enough time to explore, but leaves room for a relaxed afternoon back in Victoria.
📌 Check tide tables before going — low tide is essential! Wear sturdy, closed-toe shoes with good grip.
🔗 Visitor Guide

Victoria Butterfly Gardens
Megan’s Pick

Must-See

Ferry + Capilano
Capilano Suspension Bridge — 137 metres above the Capilano River, North Vancouver
Morning
📦

Afternoon

Capilano Suspension Bridge Park
Book Now!
Walk the iconic 137-metre suspension bridge swaying above the Capilano River gorge. Then explore the Treetops Adventure (suspension bridges through old-growth forest canopy) and the thrilling Cliffwalk along granite canyon walls. Budget 1-2 hours here — focused on the main bridge and a quick treetop walk after a long travel day.
⚠️ BUY TICKETS IN ADVANCE online — timed entry fills up fast in September!
🔗 capbridge.com
Late Afternoon / Evening
🏠

Dinner — Ramen Danbo
Travel Day Treat
Authentic Fukuoka-style tonkotsu ramen. Rich, deeply flavored pork bone broth, fully customizable. A satisfying and deeply restorative end to a long travel day.

⏰ Start: 8:00 AM
Vancouver Icons

Fuel up for a full Vancouver day.

~9:00 AM — Drive Across Lions Gate Bridge
~20 minutes from North Vancouver into Stanley Park.
🌳
Stanley Park — Vancouver
Highlight
One of North America’s greatest urban parks. Walk or cycle the 9 km seawall with stunning views of English Bay and the North Shore mountains. Visit the totem poles, explore old-growth forest, and soak in the autumn foliage.
🔗 Stanley Park Info
Lunch / Wine Hour
🍷
The Teahouse Restaurant — Stanley Park
Reserve!
A historic English Bay landmark inside Stanley Park. Stunning ocean and mountain views. Recommended for brunch or the wine hour — dinner is pricey but the setting is unmatched.
📌 RESERVATIONS RECOMMENDED.
🔗 Reserve at vancouverdine.com
Afternoon / Evening
🚗
Drive to Granville Island
About 15 minutes from Stanley Park.
🍺
Granville Island Brewing
Flexible Meal
Canada’s original craft brewery (est. 1984). Lunch OR dinner at the taproom — use flexibly depending on how the Teahouse pacing goes. While you’re on Granville Island, the famous Public Market and artisan shops are right there to explore.
🔗 granvilleislandbrewing.ca
🏠
Return to North Vancouver VRBO
Wind down after a big Vancouver day.

Vancouver Cafe + Steamworks + Night Market
⏰ Start: 8:00 AM
City Day
Richmond Night Market — one of North America’s largest night markets, Richmond, BC
Morning — Slow Start
☕
8:00 AM — Morning Cafe
TBD
A deliberately slow morning — find a cafe near the VRBO or head into Vancouver for a scenic morning. Busy evening ahead, so lean into the calm.
Midday — Explore Vancouver
🏗️
Vancouver at Leisure
Suggestions: Gastown (historic cobblestone streets, the steam clock, indie shops), Downtown / Waterfront (Canada Place, the harbour, views of the mountains). Just wander and enjoy.
Afternoon / Early Evening
🍕
Steamworks Brew Pub — Gastown
Megan’s Poutine!
A landmark craft brewpub in Gastown’s historic cobblestone district. Megan’s must-have: POUTINE. Craft beers on tap, great food, and a perfect excuse to explore Gastown’s heritage architecture.
🔗 steamworks.com
Evening — 3 to 4 Hours
🌅
Richmond Night Market
Highlight
One of North America’s largest and most vibrant night markets. Hundreds of food stalls, games, live entertainment, and dazzling lights. An unforgettable sensory experience — Asian street food, international bites, and festive atmosphere. Budget 3-4 hours to really enjoy it.
⚠️ NOTE: Confirm Wednesday availability — the market typically runs Friday–Sunday. If Wed is not available, shift to an available Fri/Sat.
🔗 richmondnightmarket.com
