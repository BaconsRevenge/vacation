## What to Pack

The Pacific Northwest in September is beautiful but unpredictable - think layers, not just a single jacket.

- **Layers are your best friend** - mornings can be cool and crisp, afternoons warm and sunny
- **Rain jacket** - non-negotiable. This is the Pacific Northwest.
- **Comfortable walking shoes** - we will be on our feet a lot (tidal pools, gardens, seawall)
- **Sturdy shoes for Botanical Beach** - the rocky terrain is no joke
- **Passports** - required for all travelers at every border crossing
- **Canadian cash (CAD)** - handy for markets, small vendors, and tips
- **Credit/debit cards** - widely accepted everywhere; most places take USD too, but you'll get a better rate paying in CAD
- **Sunscreen** - yes, even in September
- **Motion sickness remedies** - if you're sensitive to ferry crossings (two of them on this trip!)
- **Camera** - you're going to want it

> **Currency note:** Canada uses Canadian dollars (CAD). As of 2026, the exchange rate generally favors USD. Most restaurants, shops, and attractions accept major credit cards. Having some CAD cash on hand is useful for markets and small vendors. ATMs are widely available.

## Day 3 - Saturday, September 13 🌊

> **Start Time: 8:00 AM**

**Botanical Beach - Tide Pool Morning**

Today we're driving out to Port Renfrew to visit Botanical Beach Provincial Park - one of the most spectacular tidal pool destinations in all of North America. We're budgeting about 3 hours at the pools rather than a full day, which leaves us a relaxed afternoon and evening back in Victoria.

### Morning - Head West

- **8:00 AM** - Coffee [TBD - Victoria morning coffee shop]
- **~8:30 AM** - Depart Victoria for Port Renfrew (~1.5 hr drive along the scenic West Coast Road)
- **~10:00 AM** - Arrive at Botanical Beach Provincial Park

### Midday - [Botanical Beach Provincial Park](https://vancouverislandbucketlist.com/experiences/botanical-beach/)

Port Renfrew, BC

World-class tidal pools carved into ancient sandstone shelves along the dramatic Pacific coastline. At low tide, the pools come alive with sea stars, anemones, urchins, chitons, and all manner of intertidal marine life. The scenery is rugged and stunning - crashing waves, old-growth forest meeting the ocean, and that wild Pacific energy.

**Plan on about 3 hours at the tide pools** (not an all-day affair - we want to leave time to enjoy Victoria in the afternoon).

**A few things to know:**
- Check the tide tables before we go - low tide is the magic window
- Wear sturdy, closed-toe shoes - the rocks are slippery and uneven
- Bring layers - it can be windy and cool even on a warm day
- No dogs allowed in the tidal pool area

### Afternoon - Back to Victoria

- **~1:00 PM** - Depart Port Renfrew, return to Victoria (~1.5 hrs)

### Morning - [Victoria Butterfly Gardens](https://butterflygardens.com/)

Brentwood Bay, BC

Megan's pick - a lush indoor tropical conservatory where hundreds of exotic butterflies fly freely around you. Exotic birds, tropical plants, and a genuinely magical atmosphere.

### Afternoon - [The Butchart Gardens](https://butchartgardens.com/)

Brentwood Bay, BC (~2 km from Butterfly Gardens)

One of the most famous gardens in the world, and for good reason. Created over 100 years ago in a reclaimed limestone quarry, Butchart features the stunning Sunken Garden, the Rose Garden, the Japanese Garden, and the Italian Garden. In September, the gardens are in full late-summer bloom.

**Megan's highlight: Afternoon Tea at the Dining Room Restaurant**
This is the one to book in advance. The afternoon tea at Butchart Gardens is a classic experience - finger sandwiches, scones, pastries, and tea in a beautiful setting. Don't miss it. **Book as soon as possible.**

> **Pacing note:** Budget **2-3 hours total** between the two gardens. Enjoy it, but don't try to see every last corner - this is meant to be a lovely, relaxed day.

### Afternoon - [Capilano Suspension Bridge Park](https://www.capbridge.com/)

North Vancouver, BC

One of the most iconic experiences in all of British Columbia. The Capilano Suspension Bridge stretches 137 meters across the Capilano River gorge, 70 meters above the rushing water below. Beyond the bridge, the park also features:

- **Treetops Adventure** - a series of suspension bridges through the old-growth forest canopy
- **Cliffwalk** - a cantilevered walkway along the granite cliff face
- **Old-growth forest trails** - towering Douglas firs and western red cedars

> **Pacing note:** Budget **1-2 hours here**. After a ferry crossing and a long travel day, we're focusing on the main bridge and a quick treetop walk - not a deep-dive visit.

**Buy tickets online in advance** - it saves time and money at the gate.

## Day 6 - Tuesday, September 16 🌲

> **Start Time: 8:00 AM**

**Stanley Park + The Teahouse + Granville Island**

Our first full Vancouver day, and we're going to make the most of it. Stanley Park in the morning, The Teahouse for lunch or wine hour, then over to Granville Island for craft beer and the Public Market.

### Morning

- **8:00 AM** - Coffee [TBD]
- **~9:00 AM** - Drive across the Lions Gate Bridge to Stanley Park (~20 min)

### Morning/Midday - [Stanley Park](https://vancouver.ca/parks-recreation-culture/stanley-park.aspx)

Vancouver, BC

One of the greatest urban parks in the world. Stanley Park is a 405-hectare peninsula of old-growth forest right on the edge of downtown Vancouver. Stroll the famous seawall with views of the mountains and city skyline, visit the totem poles, and just soak it all in.

### Lunch/Brunch or Wine Hour - [The Teahouse Restaurant](https://www.vancouverdine.com/teahouse/)

Inside Stanley Park, Vancouver

A historic converted military building tucked inside Stanley Park with stunning views of English Bay and the North Shore mountains. Dinner here gets pricey - **brunch or the wine hour is the move**. Great food, incredible setting, and a perfect mid-day break from the park. **Reservations recommended.**

### Afternoon - Drive to Granville Island (~15 min)

### Afternoon/Evening - [Granville Island Brewing](https://www.granvilleislandbrewing.ca/en-CA)

Granville Island, Vancouver

Canada's original craft brewery (established 1984). Lunch OR dinner at the taproom - use this flexibly depending on how the Teahouse pacing goes. Waterfront views, great atmosphere, and while you're on Granville Island, the famous Public Market and artisan shops are right there to explore.

### Evening

- Return to North Vancouver VRBO, wind down after a big Vancouver day

**Megan's must-have: POUTINE.** A landmark craft brewpub in Gastown's historic cobblestone district. Craft beers on tap, great food, and a great excuse to explore Gastown's heritage architecture. ~25-30 min from North Vancouver.

### Evening - [Richmond Night Market](https://www.richmondnightmarket.com/)

Richmond, BC (~30-40 min from North Vancouver)

One of the largest night markets in North America. Hundreds of food vendors, games, entertainment, and lights. It's a sensory overload in the best possible way - the food alone is worth the drive.

> **Pacing note:** Budget **3-4 hours** at the night market. That's enough time to eat your way through it, shop, take photos, and really enjoy it.

> **Important:** The Richmond Night Market runs **Friday through Sunday evenings, May through October**. September 17 is a **Wednesday** - confirm current schedule on their website.
