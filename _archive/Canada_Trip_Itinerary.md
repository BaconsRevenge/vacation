### [Botanical Beach Provincial Park](https://vancouverislandbucketlist.com/experiences/botanical-beach/)

Port Renfrew, BC

World-class tidal pools carved into ancient sandstone shelves along the dramatic Pacific coastline. At low tide, the pools come alive with sea stars, anemones, urchins, chitons, and all manner of intertidal marine life.

- **Check tide tables before going** — low tide is the magic window
- Wear sturdy, closed-toe shoes — the rocks are slippery and uneven
- Bring layers — it can be windy and cool even on a warm day
- No dogs allowed in the tidal pool area
- Budget ~3 hours

### [Spinnakers Gastro Brewpub & Guesthouses](https://www.spinnakers.com/)

Victoria, BC

🍺 **Kirk's / Dad's Pick** — Canada's oldest brewpub (est. 1984), right on Victoria's waterfront. Brewed-on-site beers, locally-sourced food, and an oceanfront setting. Perfect for a late lunch or early dinner after returning from Port Renfrew (~1:30–2:00 PM arrival).

- **Reservation recommended** for lunch/dinner
- Oceanfront location with harbour views
- Brewed-on-site craft beers
- Locally-sourced food menu

### Beacon Hill Park

Victoria, BC

Victoria's signature 200+ acre waterfront park — gardens, ponds, peacocks roaming freely, and sweeping ocean views from Dallas Road. A gentle, beautiful way to wind down the afternoon after a big day at the coast.

- [Beacon Hill Park Info](https://www.victoria.ca/EN/main/residents/parks/beacon-hill.html)
- 200+ acres of gardens, ponds, and open meadows
- Peacocks roam freely throughout the park
- Ocean views from Dallas Road
- No admission fee

### [Victoria Butterfly Gardens](https://butterflygardens.com/)

Brentwood Bay, BC

🦋 **Megan's Pick** — A lush indoor tropical conservatory where hundreds of exotic butterflies fly freely around you. Exotic birds, tropical plants, and a genuinely magical atmosphere. Located ~2 km from Butchart Gardens near Brentwood Bay.

### [The Butchart Gardens](https://butchartgardens.com/)

Brentwood Bay, BC (~2 km from Butterfly Gardens)

One of the most famous gardens in the world. Created over 100 years ago in a reclaimed limestone quarry. Features the stunning Sunken Garden, Rose Garden, Japanese Garden, and Italian Garden. Spectacular in September with late-season blooms.

**Megan's highlight: Afternoon Tea at the Dining Room Restaurant**
- **BOOK IN ADVANCE** — fills up months ahead
- Finger sandwiches, scones, pastries, and tea in a beautiful setting
- Classic, elegant, utterly delightful

> **Pacing note:** Budget **2–3 hours total** between the two gardens.

### [Fairmont Empress — Dining](https://www.fairmont.com/en/hotels/victoria/fairmont-empress/dining.html)

Victoria, BC — Inner Harbour

🏰 Victoria's most iconic landmark. The **Q Restaurant & Bar** or **Q Bar** is a landmark Victoria experience for evening dinner. Reservations recommended.

> **Scheduling note:** Megan's tea at Butchart's Dining Room is the planned tea experience. The Empress could be an **alternative** OR a dinner option. If you want the classic Empress tea experience instead of Butchart's tea, swap the afternoon plan. The Empress tea is world-famous in its own right.

**Dining venues at the Empress:**
- Q Restaurant & Bar — upscale Pacific Northwest cuisine
- Q Bar — cocktails and lighter fare
- The Lobby Lounge — afternoon tea (world-famous; book well in advance if substituting for Butchart's tea)

**Fallback:** Cook-in at the VRBO — after a full gardens day, a relaxed cook-in night is a perfectly lovely option too.

### [Capilano Suspension Bridge Park](https://www.capbridge.com/)

North Vancouver, BC

One of the most iconic experiences in all of British Columbia. The Capilano Suspension Bridge stretches 137 meters across the Capilano River gorge, 70 meters above the rushing water below.

- **Treetops Adventure** — suspension bridges through old-growth forest canopy
- **Cliffwalk** — cantilevered walkway along the granite cliff face
- **Old-growth forest trails** — towering Douglas firs and western red cedars
- **Buy tickets online in advance** — timed entry fills up fast in September
- Budget 1–2 hours (focused visit after a long travel day)

### [Grouse Mountain](https://www.grousemountain.com/) — Optional

North Vancouver, BC (~5 min from Capilano Bridge)

⛰️ **Optional — If Time Allows** — Located just ~5 minutes from Capilano Bridge.

- **Grouse Grind** — 2.9 km, 2–3 hour vertical hike ("Mother Nature's Stairmaster"), 853m elevation gain
- **Skyride gondola** — less-strenuous alternative with great summit views
- Wildlife refuge, lumberjack shows, hiking at the summit
- Skip if the group is tired from the travel day — it'll still be a great day without it

### [Ramen Danbo](https://ramendanbo.com/our-menu-vancouver/)

Vancouver

🍜 **Nancy's / Mom's Pick** — Authentic Fukuoka-style tonkotsu ramen. Rich, deeply flavored pork bone broth, fully customizable. A satisfying and deeply restorative end to a long travel day.

### [Stanley Park](https://vancouver.ca/parks-recreation-culture/stanley-park.aspx) + E-bike Rental

Vancouver, BC

One of the greatest urban parks in the world. 405-hectare peninsula of old-growth forest right on the edge of downtown Vancouver.

**E-bikes around the Stanley Park Seawall:**
- The 9 km seawall loop is ideal for e-bikes — much less tiring than walking the whole circuit
- Rental shops near the park entrance: **Spokes Bicycle Rentals**, **Cycle City Rentals**
- Budget 2–3 hours for a relaxed lap with photo stops
- **Consider reserving e-bikes in advance** for the morning slot — popular in September

### [The Teahouse Restaurant](https://www.vancouverdine.com/teahouse/)

Inside Stanley Park, Vancouver

Historic converted military building inside Stanley Park with stunning views of English Bay and the North Shore mountains. **Brunch or the wine hour is the recommended option** (dinner is pricey). **Reservations recommended.**

### Getting to Granville Island

Two fun options from Stanley Park:
1. **Continue on e-bikes** along the Seaside Greenway (~30 min scenic ride across False Creek)
2. **Aquabus or False Creek Ferries** (small "sea taxis") — a charming 5-minute harbour ride

Both are scenic — group's choice based on energy.

### [Granville Island Public Market](https://granvilleisland.com/public-market)

Granville Island, Vancouver

One of North America's great public markets — fresh produce, local food artisans, crafts, and grab-and-go stalls with waterfront seating. Great for a browse and a casual lunch bite. Right next to Granville Island Brewing — pair them naturally.

### [Granville Island Brewing](https://www.granvilleislandbrewing.ca/en-CA)

Granville Island, Vancouver

🍺 **Kirk's / Dad's Pick** — Canada's original craft brewery (est. 1984). Taproom and restaurant on site. Lunch OR dinner — use flexibly depending on how the Teahouse pacing goes.

### Kitsilano Beach

Vancouver, BC

Famous Vancouver beach with the iconic Kits Pool (outdoor saltwater pool), beach volleyball courts, and sweeping views of downtown Vancouver and the North Shore mountains. A perfect West Coast morning — coffee walk along the beach, soak up the energy. Right next to The Naam (Kitsilano neighborhood).

### [The Naam](https://thenaam.com/)

Kitsilano, Vancouver

Vancouver institution since 1968 — vegetarian with Thai influence, open 24 hours. Right in the Kitsilano neighborhood, just steps from the beach. A casual, leisurely brunch or lunch to contrast with the Night Market's food-stall chaos later in the evening.

### [Richmond Night Market](https://www.richmondnightmarket.com/)

Richmond, BC (~30–40 min from North Vancouver)

One of the largest night markets in North America. Hundreds of food vendors, games, entertainment, and lights. Budget **3–4 hours** to really enjoy it.

> **Important:** The Richmond Night Market runs **Friday through Sunday evenings, May through October**. September 17 is a **Wednesday** — confirm current schedule on their website. If Wednesday is not available, shift to an available Fri/Sat.

> **Important:** All travelers entering Canada must have a valid passport. US citizens do not need a visa for short stays (under 6 months) but must carry a valid passport.

### Border Crossing Tips

**Entering Canada (USA to Canada):**
- Cross at Port Angeles via ferry (Black Ball Ferry Line) — customs is handled on the Victoria side upon arrival.
- Have passports ready for all travelers.
- Declare any food items, alcohol (within limits), and gifts.
- Canada allows: 1.5L wine or 1.14L spirits or 8.5L beer per adult (duty-free).
- Firearms are heavily restricted — do not bring any.

**Returning to USA (Canada to USA):**
- Use the CBP Mobile Passport Control app or NEXUS to speed up re-entry.
- Declare all goods purchased in Canada.
- US duty-free exemption: $800 per person after 48+ hours abroad.
- Expect variable wait times at Peace Arch; check live wait times before departing.
