### Suggestions for Victoria

#### Outdoor & Nature

- **Botanical Beach Provincial Park** - Exceptional tidal pools about 1 hour west of Victoria near Port Renfrew. Best at low tide. Check tide tables before going.
- **Cattle Point / Uplands Park** - Closer tidal pool option right in Oak Bay, about 15 minutes from downtown Victoria.
- **East Sooke Regional Park** - Rugged coastal trails with ocean views; great for a half-day hike.
- **Witty's Lagoon Regional Park** - Easy trails through forest to a beach with a waterfall. Family-friendly.
- **Beacon Hill Park** - Beautiful urban park in the heart of Victoria; peacocks roam freely. Great for a morning stroll.

#### Attractions & Culture

- **The Butchart Gardens** - CONFIRMED activity on Day 3. World-famous botanical garden ~20 km north of Victoria (~30 min drive from downtown). Stunning in any season. Advance ticket purchase recommended, especially in peak season. Book afternoon tea at the Dining Room Restaurant in advance.
- **Victoria Butterfly Gardens** - CONFIRMED activity on Day 3. Indoor tropical butterfly conservatory with free-flying butterflies, exotic birds, and tropical plants. Located ~2 km from Butchart Gardens near Brentwood Bay. Megan's pick - visit in the morning before Butchart Gardens afternoon tea.
- **Royal BC Museum** - Excellent natural history and First Nations exhibits. Located right on the Inner Harbour.
- **Craigdarroch Castle** - Victorian-era mansion with panoramic city views. Self-guided tours available.
- **Fisherman's Wharf** - Colorful floating homes, fresh seafood shacks, and sea lions. Very photogenic.

#### Attractions

- **Grouse Mountain** - Gondola to the summit with views of Vancouver. Wildlife refuge, lumberjack shows, and hiking.
- **Deep Cove** - Charming waterfront village; kayak rentals, great coffee, and the famous Honey Doughnuts.
- **Lonsdale Quay Market** - Waterfront public market with local vendors, food stalls, and SeaBus access to downtown Vancouver.
- **[Richmond Night Market](https://www.richmondnightmarket.com/)** - CONFIRMED. One of North America's largest night markets (seasonal, typically May-October, Fri-Sun evenings). Located in Richmond, BC (~30-40 min drive from North Vancouver). Hundreds of food vendors, games, and entertainment. Check website for current season dates/hours.
- **Steveston Village** - Historic fishing village in Richmond; fresh seafood, cannery history, and scenic waterfront.

---

### Border Crossing Tips

**Entering Canada (USA to Canada):**
- Cross at Port Angeles via ferry (Black Ball Ferry Line) - customs is handled on the Victoria side upon arrival.
- Have passports ready for all travelers.
- Declare any food items, alcohol (within limits), and gifts.
- Canada allows: 1.5L wine or 1.14L spirits or 8.5L beer per adult (duty-free).
- Firearms are heavily restricted - do not bring any.

**Returning to USA (Canada to USA):**
- Use the CBP Mobile Passport Control app or NEXUS to speed up re-entry.
- Declare all goods purchased in Canada.
- US duty-free exemption: $800 per person after 48+ hours abroad.
- Expect variable wait times at Peace Arch; check live wait times before departing.

### Packing Suggestions - Pacific Northwest Weather

The Pacific Northwest is famously unpredictable. Even in summer, temperatures can range from cool mornings to warm afternoons, with rain possible any day.

**Clothing:**
- Layering system: base layer, mid layer (fleece or light down), waterproof outer shell
- Rain jacket (essential - not optional)
- Comfortable walking shoes and waterproof hiking boots
- Light sweater or hoodie for evenings
- Sunscreen (the sun can be strong when it does appear)

**Gear:**
- Daypack for hikes and day trips
- Reusable water bottles
- Portable umbrella
- Camera or phone with good camera
- Power bank / portable charger

**Documents & Essentials:**
- Passports (all travelers)
- Travel insurance documents
- VRBO confirmation printouts or digital copies
- Ferry reservation confirmations
- Emergency contact list
- Canadian cash (CAD) for small vendors and markets

**Health & Comfort:**
- Any prescription medications (bring extra supply)
- Motion sickness remedies if sensitive to ferry crossings
- Sunscreen and lip balm
- Insect repellent (for forest hikes)
